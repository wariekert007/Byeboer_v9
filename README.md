# ByeBoer

An offline beekeeping app for South African beekeepers, in English and Afrikaans. Built the same way as FarmLedger.

- **Hives by number:** where each hive stands (with GPS), how many supers are on it, and a log of every move, super on/off, inspection and harvest
- **Move hives:** move one or many hives to a new place; the old place stays in each hive's log
- **Harvest:** tap the supers you take from each hive (you can't take more than are on it). Hives are sorted by distance from the first hive you pick. Fill in the unprocessed litres later
- **Honey stock:** unprocessed litres (harvested or **bought** from other beekeepers) → processing → processed litres → bottles. Bottling works out the litres for you (you can still change them). Selling bottles, or raw/processed honey by the litre, on an invoice takes it off the stock
- **Honey in & out report:** harvested, bought (and from whom), processed, bottled and sold, grouped per client, per product or as totals, as PDF or Excel
- **Warnings:** suspected AFB (American foulbrood) and laying workers (Cape bees)
- **Quotes & invoices** laid out like a business form, with your logo, banking details and contact details, and a checklist of what to show on each one. Turn a quote into an invoice with one tap, record payments, and share the PDF on WhatsApp or email. Old quotes and invoices from earlier in the year can be added with their own number and date, or **imported from Excel** (your own invoice forms, or a list)
- **VAT optional:** a "Tax invoice" with 15% VAT if you are registered, a plain invoice if not
- **Travel costs optional** on each quote or invoice (km × rate). **Fuel records optional**
- **Clients** and a **services list** with your standard prices (swarm removal, inspections, pollination hire…)
- **Money:** income and expenses with beekeeping categories, slip photos and repeating payments
- **Diary** with a daily reminder, and a reminder to renew your beekeeper registration
- **Logbook** (the Odometer app, built in): start and end trips with an odometer photo that the app reads, missed trips, and an Excel logbook for SARS. Trips from the Odometer app can be brought over
- **Reports** by month or SARS tax year, saved as Excel (.xlsx) or PDF, built on the device without internet
- Backup and restore to move to a new phone, and **clear records** (income, expenses, invoices, quotes, all or by date) to start over

Everything is stored on the device only. Nothing is uploaded.
